GeoTrack by Pixelo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

GeoTrack by Pixelo (“the app”) and its web panel are work tools for companies that manage field teams (for example sales representatives who visit pharmacies and clinics). The software is made by Pixelo Design (“we”). Each company that uses it (“your company”) decides who gets an account and what is recorded; your company is the owner of the business data, and we run the service for it.

1. Who can see your data

Only people in your company who have been given the right (for example your manager) can see the records of their area in the web panel. We do not sell data and we do not show advertising.

2. What the app collects

  • Account: the e-mail address and the password your company gave you (the password is stored only as a salted hash), your role, and the team you belong to.
  • Location: the position of your phone only while you have started a work shift, about once every three minutes, and when you check in or out at a customer. On Android the app shows a visible notification and on iPhone the blue location indicator while a shift is on. Tracking stops when you end the shift. The app never tracks you outside a shift.
  • Phone and app information: a name for the phone, the platform (Android or iPhone), the app version and a push-notification token, so your company can approve your phone and send you messages.
  • Work records you enter: customers (name, type, phone numbers, address, contact person, notes), visits and visit reports, orders, payments received, product samples and follow-ups.
  • Camera: used only to scan a QR code that connects the app to your company. No photo is stored.
  • Optional Telegram: if you choose to connect Telegram, the app’s server stores the chat identifier of your private chat with your company’s bot, so company messages can reach you there. You can disconnect at any time.

3. Why we use it

  • To let you sign in and to confirm that the phone belongs to you.
  • To record visits, orders, payments and samples and to show your manager where the team is during work hours.
  • To check that a check-in really happened at the customer (the position, its accuracy and its time are compared with the customer’s location).
  • To send you work messages (push notification and, if you linked it, Telegram).
  • To keep the service secure and to find errors. We use a record of changes (an audit log) for this.

4. Who it is shared with

  • Your company (its managers, according to their role).
  • Service providers that run the app for us: a server hosting provider, Cloudflare (network and the web panel), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (to deliver push notifications, which contain the message text) and, only if you link it, Telegram (which receives the message text you are sent).
  • We disclose data if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Exact location points are kept for 30 days by default (your company can choose another period) and then deleted. Summaries of shifts, and visit, order, payment and customer records, are kept while your company uses the service, because they are its business records.
  • When your company closes an account, the person can no longer sign in. Your company can ask us to delete the data of a person or of the whole company.

6. Security

All traffic between the app, the web panel and the server uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Access is limited by role and by company, and important actions are written to an audit log. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect the data.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can end your shift at any time, which stops location tracking, and you can withdraw the location permission in the phone settings (the app then cannot start a shift).
  • You can ask your company or us to show, correct or delete the data about you.
  • You can disconnect Telegram in the web panel (Notifications).

8. Children

The app is a work tool for adults. It is not meant for people under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will change the date above and, for important changes, tell your company.
